They work. Clinical trials show 15–22% average body weight loss. Tennessee has one of the highest obesity rates in the US (~36%). ## 9. Content Rules — ACCURACY IS MANDATORY

**FDA approval status — get this right every time:**
- **Wegovy** (semaglutide 2.4mg) — FDA-approved for chronic weight management (June 2021). BMI ≥30 OR BMI ≥27 with comorbidity.
- **Ozempic** (semaglutide 0.5–2mg) — FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes (December 2017). Weight loss is OFF-LABEL.
- **Zepbound** (tirzepatide 2.5–15mg) — FDA-approved for chronic weight management (November 2023).
- **Mounjaro** (tirzepatide 2.5–15mg) — FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes (May 2022). Weight loss is OFF-LABEL.
- **Compounded semaglutide** — NOT FDA-approved. Was permitted under drug shortage provisions. FDA has been restricting compounding as shortages resolve. Always disclose this.
- **Generic semaglutide** — does not exist as an FDA-approved product.

**Never say:** "Ozempic is FDA-approved for weight loss" — this is false.
**Always say:** "Ozempic is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes; weight loss is an established off-label use."

**Cite named trials:** STEP 1–5 (semaglutide), SURMOUNT 1–4 (tirzepatide), SELECT (cardiovascular outcomes), SCALE (liraglutide historical).
